  • 01 NanoGel

    Advanced Moisture Retention & Water-Holding–Based Soil Restoration Technology

    Superior Water and Nutrient Retention Through a Porous Structure.

    Nano gel features a mesoporous structure with interconnected microscopic pores that absorb and retain water and nutrients. This structure minimizes loss even under changing environmental conditions, improving both soil moisture retention and nutrient availability.

    Controlled Slow-Release Delivery When Plants Need It.

    Like a sponge, the nano gel stores water and nutrients and gradually releases them according to the plant's growth stage. Its controlled-release system provides a stable nutrient supply while minimizing nutrient loss and overapplication

    A Foundation for High-Quality, High-Yield Crops.

    Unlike conventional fertilizers that are quickly depleted, the nano gel provides a continuous and balanced supply of nutrients. This enhances root vitality and nutrient uptake, creating a strong foundation for producing high-quality, high-yield crops.

  • 02 BioGro

    Complex Microbial Fertilizer for Climate Change Adaptation

    Enhanced Climate Stress Tolerance with Functional Microorganisms.

    Our functional microbial technology combines beneficial microorganisms derived from native medicinal herbs with proprietary microbial strains to help crops withstand heat, cold, drought, excessive moisture, and other environmental stresses while maintaining healthy growth.

    Stronger Growth Through Improved Root Development.

    Complex microorganisms promote the breakdown of organic matter and improve nutrient availability in the soil, encouraging stronger root growth and greater root density. This enhances plant vigor and supports faster recovery from growth stress.

    Reducing the Need for Pesticides with Complex Microbial Technology.

    Our complex microbial technology suppresses harmful pathogens, helping reduce the incidence of plant diseases and lowering the need for chemical pesticides. It supports eco-friendly cultivation while promoting a healthier and more sustainable soil ecosystem.

  • 03 NanoGro

    Nano Metal & Mineral-Based Materials for Cost-Effective Innovation

    Maximized Nutrient Absorption with Ultra-Nano Metal Particles.

    Manufactured with ultra-fine 20–50 nm metal particles, our technology enables cellular uptake through endocytosis, significantly improving the absorption of essential nutrients. Even a small application delivers highly effective results.

    Reduced Fertilizer Use and Improved Soil-Friendly Productivity.

    High nutrient absorption minimizes fertilizer residue in the soil, reducing fertilizer usage while helping prevent salt accumulation and soil contamination. The result is improved crop yield, higher quality, and more sustainable farming.

    A Fast and Lasting Solution for Plant Nutrient Deficiency.

    Essential micronutrients such as iron, zinc, and magnesium are delivered quickly to help correct nutrient deficiencies. Continuous absorption supports long-lasting plant health and helps prevent recurring deficiencies.
