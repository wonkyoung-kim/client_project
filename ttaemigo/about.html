Message from the CEO

Hello, I am the CEO of CURESING.

As a registered nurse, I have cared for the skin and health of
countless people. Through that experience, I came to realize
that true care begins with safety and minimal irritation.
Bathing should never harm the skin—it should be a moment of
comfort and renewal.

With this belief, we created TTAEMIGO, CURESING's gentle
exfoliation solution, designed to be safe and comfortable for
everyone, from infants to those with sensitive skin.

Inspired by the wisdom of traditional Korean bathing culture
and validated through modern scientific research, TTAEMIGO is
the first product we are proud to introduce to the global market.
Made with biodegradable materials and supported by rigorous
clinical testing, we are committed to delivering only honest, safe,
and high-quality products.

At CURESING, we will continue to stand by you, making every bathing
experience cleaner, healthier, and more comfortable.

Thank you.
