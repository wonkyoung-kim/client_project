    Made with 100% Jeju-grown mandarins, Jeju Citrus Delight contains no added sugar, preservatives, or artificial additives. Carefully dried to preserve the fruit's natural sweetness and nutrition, each pouch provides the equivalent of approximately 2–3 fresh mandarins, making it a wholesome and delicious snack.

    Using SWEET GOBACK's patented near-infrared drying technology, our mandarins retain their natural flavor, aroma, and soft, chewy texture. Unlike crispy fruit chips, this is authentic dried mandarin that delivers a richer taste and a more satisfying bite.

    Ready to eat straight from the pouch—no peeling required. Enjoy it as a convenient snack on its own, or add it to yogurt, cereal, smoothies, or juice for an extra burst of fresh Jeju mandarin flavor.
