  • The Evolution of Tumblers

    As awareness of environmental protection and personal hygiene continues to grow, tumblers have become an everyday essential. Beyond simply holding beverages, the market for smart tumblers equipped with innovative features that enhance user convenience is expanding rapidly.

    Current Smart Tumbler Technology.

    Today's smart tumblers maintain beverages at a preset temperature and notify users when the drink reaches the ideal drinking temperature. Connected to a smartphone app, they offer temperature control, hydration reminders, and drink tracking. Some models also feature UV sterilization for enhanced hygiene.

    AI-Based Personalized Services.

    AI can analyze users' drinking habits, beverage preferences, and health data to recommend the ideal drink type and temperature. It can also support AI-powered customer service, review analysis, personalized marketing, and data- driven beverage recommendations.

    ALLCOM's Vision.

    ALLCOM goes beyond manufacturing smart tumblers by pursuing people-centered innovation under the vision of "All the Comfort." Starting with a portable Peltier-based beverage temperature controller for both heating and cooling, we aim to develop AI- powered smart devices and services that deliver personalized user experiences.
