Company Introduce

Thank you for visiting AllCOM Co., Ltd.


The name AllCOM stands for “All-COMFORT,” reflecting our founding philosophy of providing comfort in every moment.


Inspired by the inconvenience and hygiene issues experienced by parents, we developed the world’s first portable baby formula pot that can be controlled through a mobile application.


With medical-grade SUS-316 stainless steel, BPA-free materials, a detachable and washable structure, and smartphone-linked temperature control, our product delivers hygiene, convenience, and design excellence for new parents and childcare users.


Looking ahead, AllCOM will continue to expand its technology beyond baby care into healthcare, leisure, and lifestyle electronics, growing into a specialized global company in temperature control.


Sincerely, Seung Hyeok Jang CEO, AllCOM Co., Ltd.
