A Total K-Beauty Solutions Company Connecting Education, Products, Devices, and Global Export

LUMI KOREA is a total K-Beauty solutions company founded on 19 years of hands-on experience in the beauty industry, extensive educational expertise, and the professional knowledge of its founder, who holds a Ph.D. in Health Science.

BYLUMI, the brand of LUMI KOREA, reflects the value of beauty, people, and technology.

Our greatest value lies not simply in selling products, but in creating meaningful change through improved skin health, the professional growth of beauty experts, and the global expansion of the K-Beauty industry.

  • Today, our business includes productmdevelopment, beauty devices education, franchises, and global export.

    Through flagship products, including the BYLUMI All-in-One Cream, Timeless All-in-One Ampoule, and Air Shot Plus, we have expanded into global markets, including China, while building an integrated K-Beauty ecosystem connecting treatments, education, home care, and distribution.

  • 01 BYLUMI All-in-One Cream

    A functional cosmetic that helps improve skin brightening and reduce the appearance of wrinkles.

    BYLUMI All-in-One Cream is a dual- functional cosmetic designed to help brighten the skin and improve the appearance of wrinkles. It provides deep hydration, soothes sensitive skin, and helps maintain a healthy skin barrier. In addition, it supports skin elasticity, offering a convenient all-in-one solution for consumers seeking simple yet effective daily skincare.

    BYLUMI All-in-One Cream eliminates the need for multiple skincare steps such as toner, essence, and cream. Developed to deliver hydration, nourishment, elasticity, and ski barrier care in a single product, it offers a smooth, comfortable application while minimizing skin irritation. Its convenience and effectiveness have earned high customer satisfaction among consumers with busy lifestyles.

    BYLUMI products are also used in professional education and skincare programs in China. Beyond supplying cosmetics, we provide an integrated K-Beauty solution that combines premium products with professional training. This unique business model strengthens partnerships with overseas professionals and creates new opportunities for sustainable growth in the global K-Beauty market.

  • 02 Air Shot Plus Device

    A professional aesthetic device utilizing a non-invasive high-pressure jet delivery system.

    Air Shot Plus is designed to deliver active ampoule ingredients evenly and effectively into the skin. With 10 adjustable intensity levels, it enables beauty professionals to provide safe and precise treatments tailored to each client's skin condition, sensitivity, and skincare needs.

    Its non-invasive high-pressure jet technology minimizes discomfort while supporting effective ingredient delivery. Suitable for professional aesthetic treatments, it offers precise performance and can be adapted to a wide range of skin types and treatment goals.

    Designed with ergonomics and user convenience in mind, Air Shot Plus provides a comfortable grip, easy ampoule installation, and intuitiv intensity control. Its safety-focused design ensures reliable, efficient performance, making it an ideal device for professional aesthetic clinics and skincare centers.
