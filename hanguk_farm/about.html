CEO's Message

Greetings,


Since its establishment in 2013, HANGUK FARM has remained committed to excellence, driven by open-minded thinking, creative ideas, and a spirit of innovation. For more than a decade, we have continuously developed products while applying our proprietary manufacturing technology to minimize nutrient loss, preserve the natural flavor of our ingredients, and ensure safe, reliable food from production through distribution.


Building on this foundation, we are striving to become a leading global food company through active marketing strategies in the increasingly competitive international market. Rather than settling for our current achievements, we will continue to pursue change and innovation with the vision of becoming a company that thrives for the next 100 years. We sincerely appreciate your continued trust and support, and we will repay it through sustainable growth and unwavering commitment to quality.


Thank you.

Management Philosophy

Strict Quality Control for Safe Organic Food

We are committed to delivering healthy and safe food through rigorous quality management. Our products are manufactured under the HACCP-certified food safety system, with thorough inspections for pesticide residues, harmful heavy metals, and country of origin, ensuring trusted quality from production to consumers.

Convenience You Can Enjoy Anytime

Jam and collagen are packaged in convenient stick and pouch formats, making them easy to enjoy anytime, anywhere. A quick and hassle-free way to enjoy your favorite products wherever you go.

From Cultivation to Production and Distribution

From cultivation and production to distribution, we are committed to delivering food you can trust. With honest practices and an efficient distribution system, we provide safe, high-quality products quickly and reliably.
