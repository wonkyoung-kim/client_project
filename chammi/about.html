Company History

  • 2012

    Incorporated as Chammi Food Co., Ltd.

    Began exporting to Australia and the United States.

    Selected as a HIT 500 Company by the Korea SMEs and Startups Agency (KOSME).

  • 2013

    Constructed a new manufacturing facility and relocated the headquarters.

    Obtained ISO 9001 (Quality Management System) Certification.

    Initiated HACCP Certification Preparation.

  • 2014

    Began supplying in-flight meals to Jeju Air Started exports to Russia, Singapore, Hong Kong and Mongolia.

    Certified as an Enterprise with a Dedicated Research and Development Department.

  • 2015

    Obtained HACCP Certification.

    Supplied Type-S Combat Rations to the Republic of Korea Army.

    Participated in the World Korean Business Convention.

  • 2016

    Received the Outstanding Trademark Award from the President of the Korea Institute of Patent Information.

    Certified as a Clean Workplace.

    Ramen & Rice Selected as a HIT 500 Product.

  • 2017

    Began supplying in-flight meals to T’way Air.

  • 2018

    Launched Hot & Cook (3 product varieties).

    Began exports to Taiwan.

    Registered manufacturing facility with the U.S. FDA.

  • 2019

    Signed a Women-Friendly Workplace Agreement.

  • 2020

    Certified under Sillarian, Gyeongbuk’s Joint Brand for Outstanding SMEs.

    Obtained Sillarian Certification.

    Selected as an Outstanding Job Creation Company in Gyeongsangbuk-do.

    Received the Excellence Award at the 2020 Rice Processed Food Awards.

  • 2021

    Named a Top 10 Korean Rice Processed Food Product and Received the Minister of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs Award.

    Obtained ISO 22000 and FSSC 22000 Certifications.

    Certified as a Small and Venture Business.

    Obtained INNOBIZ (Innovative SME) Certification.

    Received the Excellence Award at the Gyeongsangbuk-do SME Awards.

  • 2022

    Obtained Family-Friendly Company Certification.

  • 2023

    Participated as a Sponsor of the World Vision Global 6K Hiking 2023.

    Supported World Vision’s Clean Water and Sanitation Program in Africa.
