With customer satisfaction as our highest priority, all of our employees work together as one team to deliver our very best.

Chammi Food Co., Ltd. is a company specializing in instant dehydrated food products. Since our establishment in 2004, we have continuously invested our resources and efforts into product development and advanced manufacturing facilities to provide healthy, safe, and convenient food solutions for over a decade.

With fresh ideas and innovative thinking,
we will continue striving to grow and reach new heights together with our valued customers.

Today and in the future, we will continue to place customer satisfaction at the heart of everything we do. We sincerely ask for your continued support, encouragement, and trust, so that all of our employees can work together as one and always give their very best.

  • 01 A Growing Company

    Built on a strong foundation and solid capabilities, we have continued to move forward with confidence and stability. Since the launch of our self-heating meal brand Hot & Cook, we have achieved a threefold increase in sales within just four years.

  • 02 Beyond Korea, Reaching the World

    As a leading brand in the instant dehydrated food industry, Chammi Food is expanding beyond Korea and growing into a global company through continuous and steady export activities.


    Driven by our commitment to quality and innovation, we continue to strengthen our presence in international markets and establish ourselves as a trusted global leader in instant dehydrated food products.

  • 01 The Four Core Management Principles That Drive Chammi Food

    At Chammi Food, we maximize customer value through four core management principles: Quality Management, Environmental Management, Ethical Management, and Social Contribution. These principles guide every aspect of our business, enabling us to deliver excellence, promote sustainability, uphold integrity, and contribute positively to society.

    Ethical Management.

    We uphold a strong commitment to ethical management by adhering to fundamental principles and standards. Through responsible business practices, we strive to fulfill our corporate social responsibilities while fostering mutual growth and development with our customers, employees, and business partners.

    Quality Management.

    Through continuous quality management and improvement, we have earned recognition for excellence in both domestic and international markets. Our commitment to the highest quality standards provides customers with confidence and trust in our products.

    Social Contribution.

    As part of our sharing and giving philosophy, we actively engage in social contribution initiatives that support local communities and promote a culture of care and compassion. Through these efforts, we strive to become a company that is respected and trusted by society.

    Environmental Management.

    Recognizing environmental protection as a core corporate responsibility, we implement and maintain an effective environmental management system to promote sustainable practices and minimize our environmental impact.

  • 02 A Wide Variety of Delicious Products

    Discover Chammi Food’s range of healthy, convenient, and safe food products, carefully crafted to deliver both great taste and quality you can trust.

    Enjoy a Hot Meal Anywhere.

    Hot & Cook is a self-heating meal that lets you enjoy hot food anytime, anywhere. Just add water and wait 10 minutes. Our menu includes Bibimbap, Ramen & Rice, Gukbap, and Rice Cake Soup.

    Instant Bibimbap and Ramen & Rice.

    Simply add hot water to enjoy authentic Korean flavors. Choose from a variety of Bibimbap options and four Ramen & Rice flavors, including Jjamppong, Kimchi Stew, and Beef Seaweed Soup.

    Convenient and Nutritious Grain Shakes.

    Made with seven Korean-grown grains and high-protein ingredients, our grain shakes offer a quick and healthy meal option in Oat, Black Soybean, and Green Tea flavors.
