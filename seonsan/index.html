A 500-Year-Old Family Brew of the Seonsan Kim Clan in Gyeongsang Province

Premium Seonsan Yakju with 500 Years of Tradition Crafted the Traditional Way, Preserving the Authentic Taste.

SEONSAN

SEONSAN is a brand that reinterprets forgotten traditions with a modern perspective, embracing the philosophy of Beopgo Changsin (法古創新)— honoring the old while creating the new—to build a sustainable business model.

  • From 1413 until 1995, SEONSAN was the administrative district covering what is now the Gumi area of Gyeongsangbuk-do, Korea.

    The name SEONSAN has been used since the reign of King Taejong of the Joseon Dynasty. It was known as a peaceful place, free from banditry and blessed with warm, welcoming community. As the birthplace of Seonsan Yakju and many distinguished scholars, including Kim Jong-jik, it earned the reputation as the cradle of Yeongnam's great figures.

  • Seonsan Original

    Seonsan Original is a Samyangju crafted with a bright, yogurt-like acidity, delivering a refreshing and well-balanced flavor profile.

    1. Brand Philosophy

    To ensure the finest quality, we use 100% Seonsan nonglutinous rice and glutinous rice grown along the Nakdong River in Gyeongsangbuk-do. This creates a depth of flavor unmatched by ordinary makgeolli. Using only the finest local ingredients, we faithfully recreate the taste of a premium family- brewed liquor passed down for over 500 years— this is the value and philosophy of Seonsan Brewery.

    2. Tasting Notes

    Seonsan Original is distinguished by its vibrant acidity and rich full-bodied character. It pairs exceptionally well with a wide variety of dishes and is best enjoyed on the rocks, served chilled over ice.

    3. Brewing Method.

    To preserve the authentic, full-bodied character of Seonsan Brewery's original brew, Seonsan Original is made with 100% traditional nuruk and undergoes long-term fermentation and aging. The result is a thick, rich unfiltered rice wine with exceptional depth and complexity.
