TWELL is a company dedicated to researching plant resources and developing functional ingredients.

TWELL is a specialized functional pet food company that develops science-based nutritional solutions for disease-specific health management in companion animals, using naturally derived functional ingredients.

TWELL's identity is rooted in the development of functional ingredients.

While TWELL is currently recognized as a pet food brand, our true vision extends far beyond pet nutrition. We aim to become a platform company that develops plant-derived functional ingredients and applies them across a wide range of industries, creating innovative solutions and sustainable value through advanced functional material technologies.

  • Creating Better Nutrition for Pets with Health Conditions

    At TWELL, we develop customized nutritional solutions for pets with health conditions. Using naturally derived functional ingredients, we create pet food, treats, and supplements backed by scientific research. With our in-house manufacturing facilities and OEM/ODM capabilities, we are expanding as a trusted pet healthcare brand in both domestic and global markets.
