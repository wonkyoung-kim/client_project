Welcome Message

Solutions for Healthier Lives of Companion Animals

Welcome to TWELL.


TWELL is dedicated to providing innovative solutions that support the health and well-being of companion animals. Backed by scientific research and advanced R&D, we develop premium pet nutrition tailored to the unique nutritional needs of pets.


Through our distinctive brands—NATURE ALO, PREVETCO, BONIFF, and REPTQ—we are committed to creating a healthier future for companion animals by delivering trusted, science-based nutritional solutions.

Backed by R&D Expertise

Built on extensive R&D expertise and a rigorous quality management system, TWELL collaborates with domestic and global partners to lead the companion animal market.


Through our OEM and ODM partnerships, we provide customized total solutions—from innovative product development to reliable manufacturing and stable supply.

A Trusted Partner for Every Journey

TWELL is more than a pet food manufacturer—we are a trusted partner dedicated to supporting the lifelong health and well-being of companion animals and the people who care for them.


As a global leader in healthy pet nutrition, we will continue to drive innovation through ongoing research and development, creating greater value and contributing to a healthier future for companion animals.
