Creating Sustainable Foods, Delivering Better Health.

CHAMING is a natural fermentation and health food brand that grows in harmony with nature.

CHAMING's journey began not with the simple goal of manufacturing food, but with a meaningful question: "How can we create food that benefits both people and nature?"


Guided by this belief, we have remained committed to using trusted eco-friendly agricultural ingredients while developing patented fermentation technologies that enhance their natural value. Through continuous innovation, we strive to create healthier, more meaningful food products that people can enjoy with confidence.


These steady efforts have earned CHAMING recognition as a Certified Preliminary Social Enterprise. As a women-owned venture company, we continue to grow through innovation, craftsmanship, and a commitment to sustainability, creating lasting value for both people and the environment.

Core Technology

Using beneficial microorganisms isolated from traditional fermented foods, CHAMING ferments carefully selected botanical ingredients—including jujube, wild pear (Sandolbae), oriental raisin tree, red ginseng, and dendropanax—to enhance their functional properties. Through this patented fermentation technology, we develop innovative food ingredients with improved bioactive compounds and continue to create high-quality functional food products that combine traditional wisdom with modern scientific innovation.
