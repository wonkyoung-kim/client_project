  • 01.Cooling Accounts for 40% of AI Data Center OPEX

    As server performance continues to advance, managing the resulting heat generation has become one of the most critical challenges in data center operations.

  • 02.End-to-End Thermal Management Architecture — From Chipset to Facility

    Ninelabs' expertise goes far beyond individual components. We deliver a fully integrated thermal management ecosystem that safely transfers heat generated at the chipset level through UQDs and manifolds, processes it via an AI-driven CDU, and efficiently dissipates it through a BIM-optimized facility network.

  • Products

    Cold Plates | Server Racks | Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs) | Modular Data Centers (MDCs) | Cooling Pipeline Infrastructure

    Cold Plate

    Engineered for high-performance AI and computing applications, our Cold Plates deliver up to 1,200W of cooling capacity with over 90% thermal efficiency. Integrated leak detection sensors and a composite leak-protection barrier provide enhanced operational safety, while customized flow-channel designs optimized for each chipset's thermal profile maximize heat dissipation and cooling performance.

    Server Rack

    Designed with a one-touch integrated rack architecture, our Server Racks simplify high-density server deployment and piping connections. Built to industry standards for seamless scalability and compatibility, they feature optimized coolant flow paths and advanced heat-distribution technology to support efficient thermal management in high-performance computing environments.

    CDU (Coolant Distribution Unit)

    Our CDU enables efficient thermal management through real-time PUE monitoring and AI-driven automated control algorithms that optimize energy consumption. Built to meet the stringent quality standards of leading global enterprises, it offers proven reliability, OEM supply capabilities, and advanced manufacturing expertise for scalable deployment in AI data centers.

    MDC (Modular Data Center)

    Our MDC solutions are available in a range of form factors—including modular buildings, skids, and containerized systems—to meet diverse site requirements. Factory-prefabricated construction minimizes deployment time while ensuring consistent quality and reliability. By integrating proprietary liquid cooling and precision control technologies across servers, power systems, and fire protection infrastructure, our MDC delivers exceptional energy efficiency, operational simplicity, and performance for high-density computing environments.

    Pipeline Infrastructure

    Our high-precision piping systems are engineered with hygienic design standards and factory-prefabricated construction to reduce installation time while ensuring superior airtightness and reliability. Integrated leak detection and water quality management systems provide enhanced operational safety, delivering a secure, efficient, and highly reliable cooling infrastructure optimized for high-performance and AI computing environments.
