WOONJIN combines the Korean words for
“destiny” and “life.”

Destiny represents connection and enduring values that stand the test of time, while life symbolizes a journey of challenges, achievements, and continuous growth. These meanings are expressed through a circular pattern, reflecting the philosophy and identity of WOONJIN.
