  • 01 TEANIQUE

    A convenient powdered stick tea developed to make traditional Ssanghwa tea more enjoyable for everyone, combining its time-honored wellness benefits with the sweet, refreshing flavor of peach.

    Golden Blend with Peach

    The rich, full-bodied character of traditional Ssanghwa tea is elevated with the delicate sweetness and refreshing notes of peach. This carefully balanced blend preserves the authentic depth of Ssanghw while creating a smoother, lighter taste that can be enjoyed by everyone. The result is a satisfying and approachable drinking experience for all ages.

    Trust Built with Local Growers.

    After learning the value of nature- based farming in Jeju, a young farmer returned to her hometown of Yeongcheon with a vision to cultivate better produce. Through dedication and care, she grows peaches that capture the natural flavor and aroma of the region. We carefully select these peaches and incorporate them into our products, delivering the freshness of the orchard and the honesty of local agriculture to our customers.

    Crafted with 8 Traditional Ssanghwa Ingredients

    Our blend is made with eight traditional Ssanghwa ingredients— Angelica Root, Cnidium, Cinnamon, Ginger, Astragalus, Rehmannia Root, Jujube, and Licorice—carefully combined to create the distinctive depth and richness of traditional Ssanghwa tea. The addition of peach softens the flavor profile with gentle sweetness and refreshing fruit notes, resulting in a harmonious blend that is both authentic and easy to enjoy.

  • 02 RUGTOR

    A mental performance product formulated with ingredients such as L-theanine, designed to support sustained focus and provide convenient energy replenishment for active lifestyles.

    Lighter, Smoother in Liquid Form

    Our liquid energy gel delivers a smooth, easy-to-consume experience that fits seamlessly into both active and everyday lifestyles. With its clean taste, non-sticky texture, and convenient portability, it is designed for quick and effortless energy replenishment whenever and wherever you need it.

    Refreshing Shine Muscat Aroma

    Made with the bright aroma and naturally sweet flavor of Shine Muscat grapes grown in Yeongcheon, a region known for its abundant sunshine and pristine environment. The refreshing taste complements NeuroFlow perfectly, creating a delicious and uplifting experience before, during, or after physical activity.

    Caffeine-Free Formula

    Rather than relying on short-term stimulation, NeuroFlow is designed to support balanced energy and consistent performance throughout the day. Completely caffeine-free, it offers a more comfortable and approachable option for those seeking sustained focus and everyday wellness without the jitters often associated with caffeine.
