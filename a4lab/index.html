  • 01 XAVE Prime

    XAVE provides real-time 3D visualization and 1 mm precision navigation, improving surgical accuracy, efficiency, and clinical outcomes.

    Advanced Non-Invasive Position Tracking System

    Designed to maximize surgical precision, this non-invasive tracking system provides accurate reference coordinates without invasive fixation. Real-time high-precision position tracking supports safer procedures, reliable navigation, and enhanced clinical confidence throughout treatment.

    Screw-Free Fixation System

    This screw-free fixation system enables rapid setup, improved patient comfort, and secure positioning throughout procedures. By reducing unnecessary handling and simplifying workflow, it enhances efficiency while maintaining high levels of accuracy and reproducibility.

    Real-Time Remote Expert Support System

    A real-time remote support solution that allows experts to monitor live video and device data during procedures. Immediate guidance and feedback help improve accuracy, support clinical decision-making, and enhance patient safety and treatment outcomes.

  • 02 XAVE Air

    XAVE Air delivers precise, fast, and intuitive navigation in a compact and cost-effective platform. Designed for small operating rooms and educational settings, it provides reliable guidance, streamlined workflows, and easy operation without compromising accuracy or performance.

    Portable High-Precision Tracking & Navigation

    Bringing precision into a portable form, XAVE Air combines high-accuracy surgical tracking with an attachment-based navigation system. It delivers reliable accuracy across diverse clinical environments while improving procedural efficiency, safety, mobility, and ease of use for healthcare professionals.

    Korea’s First. A New Standard

    Korea’s first mini-model for surgical navigation delivering high-precision, real-time tracking and improved workflow efficiency modern operating room.

    Real-Time 3D Surgical Path Simulation

    Powered by real-time 3D surgical path simulation, XAVE Air helps clinicians quickly assess changing surgical conditions and determine optimal pathways during procedures. This advanced navigation technology enhances accuracy, supports confident decision-making, and improves safety in complex clinical environments.
