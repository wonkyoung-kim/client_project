  • 01 Yugi Cup

    Experience the warmth of handcrafted Yugi, meticulously made by skilled artisans.

    Feature 1. A Smoother Drinking Experience

    A Yugi cup enhances the aroma and character of your drink, creating a smoother and more enjoyable tasting experience. Whether serving chilled or warm beverages, it allows you to appreciate their flavor and aroma with every sip, making each moment more refined and satisfying.

    Feature 2. Naturally Cool to the Touch

    Yugi is known for its excellent thermal conductivity, helping chilled beverages maintain a refreshing sensation for longer. Its naturally cool surface provides a pleasant feel in your hand, allowing you to enjoy a crisp and refreshing drinking experience.

    Feature 3. Naturally Hygienic Material

    Crafted from a traditional alloy of copper and tin, Yugi has been valued for generations as premium tableware. Its dense forged structure and the natural properties of copper help keep the cup clean and hygienic, making it an excellent choice for everyday use with lasting quality.

  • 02 Customizable Cup Holder

    Choose your preferred length and material to create a cup holder that perfectly suits your style.

    Feature 1. Safe Stainless Steel and Aluminum

    The holder is precision-crafted from POSCO 304 stainless steel and 6061 aluminum, materials recognized for their durability and reliability. Designed for everyday use, it resists deformation while minimizing metallic odors caused by moisture or contact with hands, providing a clean and comfortable user experience.

    Feature 2. Premium Weight and Feel

    Its carefully balanced weight delivers a refined sense of quality the moment you hold it. Neither too light nor too heavy, the ergonomic design offers a secure grip and exceptional comfort. Combined with its precise finish, it provides a premium experience with every use.

    Feature 3. Rust-Resistant Stainless Steel

    Made from high-quality stainless steel with an electropolished finish, the holder features a smooth surface that enhances durability and corrosion resistance. It is designed to maintain its clean appearance over time while offering easy maintenance and reliable performance for everyday use.
