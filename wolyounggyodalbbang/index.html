A Moon-Shaped Cream Bun Inspired by Woryeonggyo, Andong, Gyeongbuk, Filled with Love.

Woryeonggyo, the 400-Year-Old Bridge of Love. Wolyounggyo Dalppang captures the warmth of that timeless love in every bite.

Named after the moon reflected on Woryeonggyo, we call it “Dalppang” (Moon Bread).

Woryeonggyo, one of Andong's most beautiful landmarks, is a graceful wooden bridge surrounded by breathtaking scenery. As night falls, the bridge glows beneath the moonlight, creating a truly unforgettable view. Inspired by the touching love story of Woni and her husband, this iconic bridge became the inspiration for Wolyounggyo Dalppang. Why not visit Andong and experience the story, the scenery, and the taste of Wolyounggyo Dalppang for yourself?

  • We believe great bread begins with great ingredients. That’s why Wolyounggyo Dalppang is made with carefully selected, high-quality ingredients.

    Made with carefully selected organic flour, Andong yam powder, and fresh dairy cream, Wolyounggyo Dalppang delivers a smooth texture, rich flavor, and a wholesome taste in every bite.

  • Five Delightful Flavors

    Discover five irresistible cream fillings, each crafted to make every bite of Wolyounggyo Dalppang even more special.

    Red Bean Cream Bun.

    Made with finely ground whole red beans blended into smooth cream, this signature bun offers a rich yet delicate sweetness. Its light, balanced flavor makes it a favorite for all ages.

    Green Tea Cream Bun.

    Crafted with premium green tea from Jeju Island, this bun features a delicate, earthy flavor balanced by smooth cream. It is perfect for those who enjoy a clean, less-sweet dessert.

    Black Sesame Cream Bun.

    Made with premium Korean black sesame seeds roasted on the day of production, this bun delivers a deep, nutty aroma. The rich sesame flavor blends perfectly with smooth cream for a satisfying taste.

    Yogurt Cream Bun.

    Filled with refreshing yogurt cream, this bun offers a sweet, tangy flavor and a soft texture. Light and refreshing, it is a delicious treat enjoyed by both children and adults.

    Strawberry Cream Bun.

    Filled with real Korean strawberry pieces, this bun captures the fresh, fruity taste of ripe strawberries. The creamy filling and juicy fruit create a sweet and refreshing flavor in every bite.
